# 03 · Outreach

## First message (DM or email)

```
Hi [Name],

I'm [your name] from [brand]. I've been watching your [specific video/series],
especially [specific detail]. It's exactly how our audience talks about [topic].

We're running a paid partnership for [product] and would love to work with you:
- [Deliverable, e.g. 1 Reel + 3 alternative hooks]
- Paid usage as an ad from your handle for [X] days
- Fee: [range or "happy to hear your rates"]

Would you be open to a quick chat? I can send the full brief.

[Name]
```

**Why it works:** it's specific (proves you watched), states paid usage up front, and makes the next step easy.

## Follow-up (after 3–4 days)

```
Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried.
Happy to send the brief and rates over so you can decide quickly.
```

One follow-up, then move on.

## After they say yes

1. Agree rights and fees using [whitelisting-playbook / 01-rights-checklist](https://github.com/vidhyaganapathy/whitelisting-playbook/blob/main/01-rights-checklist.md)
2. Send the brief: [whitelisting-playbook / 02-creator-brief](https://github.com/vidhyaganapathy/whitelisting-playbook/blob/main/02-creator-brief.md)
3. Set the content due date and posting date
4. Confirm how permission for paid usage will be granted
