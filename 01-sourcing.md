# 01 · Sourcing

## Start with the buyer, not the creator

| Question | Example answer |
|---|---|
| Who is the buyer? | [e.g. new drivers and their parents, or tech founders] |
| Where do they spend time? | [Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn] |
| Who do they already trust? | [types of creators: educators, reviewers, peers] |
| What content format fits the product? | [demo, day-in-the-life, tutorial, review] |

## Where I find creators

- **Platform search and hashtags** in the niche
- **Meta's creator tools** (e.g. Creator Marketplace) for creators open to brand partnerships
- **Who your audience already follows:** check followers and commenters of competitors and adjacent brands
- **Creators already mentioning the category** or your product organically
- **Creators who've run partnership ads** for similar brands (they understand paid usage)

## Tiers

| Tier | Typical role |
|---|---|
| **Nano / micro** | High trust, lower cost, great for testing hooks and angles |
| **Mid-tier** | Balance of reach and trust |
| **Macro** | Reach; use once the message is proven |

Start with several smaller creators to find the winning angle, then scale.
